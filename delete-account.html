Delete Your FotoShop Account

Last updated: April 22, 2026

This page explains how users of FotoShop can request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to request account deletion

Email us at: YOUR_EMAIL@example.com

Use subject: Delete my FotoShop account

Send the request from the same email address used in your FotoShop account.

What will be deleted

  • Your account profile and authentication record
  • Your uploaded photos/images stored by the app
  • Your AI generation history and related app content
  • Associated app metadata linked to your account ID

What may be retained

  • Minimal security or legal compliance logs
  • Records required to prevent fraud or abuse

Retention period for the above limited records: up to 30 days unless a longer period is required by law.

Processing time

We process verified deletion requests within 7 business days.
