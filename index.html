FotoShop ("we", "our", "us"), developed by abdo_tech (Abdelrahman Elmanawy), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices when using the FotoShop mobile application.

We may collect the following information depending on which features you use:

Camera data: When you use the camera feature, the app accesses your device camera to capture photos.
Photos and media: When you choose images from your gallery, the app accesses selected media files.
Account information: If you sign in (for example using Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication), we may collect basic account details such as name, email, and account ID.
Cloud content: If you upload or save files to cloud services (such as Firebase Storage / Firestore), we process that content to provide app functionality.
Technical and diagnostic data: Basic device/app diagnostics and crash logs may be collected to improve performance and fix issues.
2) How We Use Information

We use information to:

Provide core app features (capture, edit, save, and manage photos)
Authenticate users and secure accounts
Sync, store, or retrieve user content in cloud services
Improve app stability, performance, and security
Respond to support requests
3) Permissions We Request

FotoShop may request:

Camera permission (android.permission.CAMERA)
Media/Storage permissions (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on older Android versions)

Permissions are used only for features you choose to use.

We do not sell your personal data.

6) Security
